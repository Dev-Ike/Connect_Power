Create your account or log in, then complete your application in five steps.

  3. Programme streamChoose one of the five programme tracks, then check your application review slip, including your NIN.
  4. Document uploadUpload your NIN, local government certificate, passport photograph, birth certificate or age declaration and CV as PDF, PNG or JPG files.

About Connect Power

We help Nigerians learn practical skills and provide jobs to the masses across five sectors.

Who we are

Connect Power is a learning and jobs platform for Nigerians. Many capable people lack access to practical training and to opportunities that match their skills, while businesses struggle to find trained, reliable people.

We close that gap by combining skills learning with job opportunities in five sectors: Tech Connect, Agro Connect, Health Connect, Build Connect and Teach Connect.

Learn practical skills

Training built around the skills each sector needs, so you finish ready to work.

Connect to real jobs

Pathways into work with partner employers and organisations that have been checked.

Open to everyone

Open to Nigerians in all 36 states and the FCT, regardless of gender, religion or ethnicity.

Our five sectors

Each programme track combines learning with a route into work.

Tech Connect

Software development, data analysis, cybersecurity and IT support.

Agro Connect

Modern farming, agribusiness, food processing and agri-tech.

Health Connect

Community health, nursing support, laboratory and pharmacy skills.

Build Connect

Construction, engineering and skilled trades such as welding, electrical work and plumbing.

Teach Connect

Modern teaching, tutoring and school administration methods.

Our mission, vision and core purpose

What we stand for and how we measure our success.

Mission

To empower individuals and organizations across Nigeria and Africa by connecting exceptional talent with meaningful opportunities, ensuring every person finds purposeful work and every employer builds a thriving team.

Vision

To shape a future where every African professional realizes their full potential through meaningful employment and every business flourishes with the right people behind it.

Core purpose

Achieve

We measure our success by the growth of our people and partners. We are dedicated to helping talent land rewarding careers and empowering organizations to reach their highest ambitions.
