FAQ & Support

Answers, contact information and help.

What is Connect Power?
Connect Power is a youth empowerment programme that connects talented Nigerians with training and opportunity across five tracks: Teach, Tech, Agro, Build and Health Connect.
Who can apply?
Any Nigerian citizen with a valid NIN who is unemployed, self-employed, a student, or employed but seeking new skills. Applicants must be able to complete the full application, including document uploads and the application fee.
What documents do I need?
You will need: Birth Certificate/Age Declaration, WAEC/NECO/NABTEB result, Passport Photograph, Government Identification Letter, and Tertiary Degree Certificate or Statement of Result. NYSC certificate, professional certificates and CV are optional.
How much is the application fee?
The application fee is ₦1,015, paid securely via Paystack at the final step. Your application is only submitted after your payment is confirmed.
Can I use the same email to sign up twice?
No. One email address can only have one account. If you try to sign up again with the same email, you will be asked to log in with that email and the password you used before.
Is my personal data safe?
Yes. Your data is handled in line with the NDPR (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation). Passwords are encrypted, your NIN is stored securely, and your documents are only seen by the application review team.

Our Mission

At Connect Power, our mission is to empower individuals and organizations to achieve their full potential by connecting talent with opportunity. We believe every person in Nigeria and Africa deserves meaningful work, and every employer deserves exceptional talent. We are dedicated to delivering quality connections and strive for excellence in matching talent to the right opportunities, resulting in long-lasting, fulfilling employment relationships.
